Describing Professions
Pattern: Subject + is/are + a/an + job
SentenceSpanish
I am a doctor.Soy médico/a.
She is a nurse.Ella es enfermera.
He is a pilot.Él es piloto.
We are teachers.Somos maestros.
They are cooks.Ellos son cocineros.
Important: In English we say "I am a teacher" — you always need the article! In Spanish you say "Soy maestra" (no article).

Alex says...
Practice jobs every day! Ask people around you: "What do you do?" and answer about yourself: "I am a student." Try describing your family: "My mother is a teacher. My father is an engineer." The more you use the Verb to Be with jobs, the more natural it feels! ¡Sigue practicando!
