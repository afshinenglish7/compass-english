Grammar Focus
Rule 1 — The Alphabet
The English alphabet has 26 letters: 5 vowels and 21 consonants.
A · E · I · O · U = vowels
El alfabeto inglés tiene 26 letras: 5 vocales y 21 consonantes.
Rule 2 — "A" vs "AN"
Use "a" before consonant sounds, "an" before vowel sounds.
a book · an apple · an umbrella
Usa "a" antes de consonantes y "an" antes de vocales.
Rule 3 — Capital Letters
Names always start with a capital letter.
My name is Alex. / I am from Mexico.
Los nombres siempre empiezan con mayúscula.

Reading Passage — The power of the alphabet

Every language has a writing system, and English uses the Latin alphabet — the same one used in Spanish, French, and many other languages. The English alphabet has 26 letters, each with an uppercase and a lowercase form.

Some letters are called vowels (A, E, I, O, U) because they make open, flowing sounds. The other 21 letters are consonants, which make more closed or blocked sounds. Every English word must contain at least one vowel!

The letter "E" is the most common in English — it appears in more words than any other letter. The least common is "Z", but it still appears in everyday words like "zero" and "zone."
