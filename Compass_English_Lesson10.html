Grammar — Possessives
Rule 1 — Possessive 's
Add 's after a name or noun to show possession.
Robert's wife is Susan. · Lucy's brother is Tom. · Tom's parents are Robert and Susan.
Añade 's después del nombre para indicar posesión.
Rule 2 — His / Her / Their
Use his for male, her for female, their for both.
His name is Robert. · Her name is Susan. · Their children are Tom, Lucy and Sam.
His = de él · Her = de ella · Their = de ellos
Rule 3 — My / Your / Our
Use my (I), your (you), our (we).
My family is big. · Your grandmother is kind. · Our parents are teachers.
My = mi(s) · Your = tu(s) · Our = nuestro/a(s)
